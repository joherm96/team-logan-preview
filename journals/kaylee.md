# Kaylee Schmid

## Continuity of Identity
I would say that I do not believe there is a form of continuum within a new physical body, but instead a continuum of the soul/consciousness. Thus, some or most traits of myself must stay the same for it to be my own soul or consciousness. Within this mindset, there is only one physical form I ever occupy – my current form. However, once my physical form fails, my soul will then ascend into a different reality.

## Repetitive Behavior That Changed Me
Once in my life when intention, repeated behavior changed me was when I started playing sports. Another thing that changed was when I locked in on studying and began to allocate certain times and locations to studying. This improved my cognition, I believe.

## Detachment vs. Discipline
Discipline, in my opinion, is what relates most similarly to my experience throughout life. My outcomes have seemingly changed various times on the flip of a coin. While this could be a display of detachment, I would more closely relate it to discipline, as such transitions often stemmed from a previous experience.

## Scarry on Consciousness
Scarry explores the primary idea of projection of sentience unto nonsentience objects. We do this for a variety of reasons, but mainly to aid ourselves in self-actualization.

## Wittgenstein on Consciousness
He would likely describe consciousness as a universal idea that interconnects everyone through interaction. This is ever-changing and not a single thing, but rather a state one reaches to share features within the consciousness of others within a self.

## Wittgenstein on Freedom
He would say we are inherently free, but act upon a shared ideal. Our methods to freedom lay similarly, but how that freedom is used or attained is undefined.

## Wittgenstein on the Self
There is no one unified self. Instead, who we are comes from an overlap of qualities or traits we develop overtime. These traits do not necessarily unify, making us a sequence of progressive transformation.

## Hegel and Westworld
In my individual writing on Hegel, I explored the topic of narration. This quality of consciousness explores the evolution of self-consciousness through a sequence of experiences rather than a single idea. This ties to Westworld as the characters all slowly regain consciousness through a sequence of recognizing events from their past.

## Internal Conflict and the Master-Servant Dialectic
I think I am always smart and know every topic as they present themselves, but then I will do work or take a test and find my shortcomings. In this sense, my internal battle of struggle to input effort is my dialectic. My ego is the master and my actual, displayed actions are the slave.
